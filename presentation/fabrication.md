**Infinite Dungeons** was designed and programmed by **Claude**, Anthropic's AI model: the engine, the rules, the monsters, the rooms, the interface… and every picture on this page, rendered by the game's own code.

- **Music** made with Suno and ElevenLabs.
- **Sound effects** made with ElevenLabs, or synthesized by the game from recipes.
- **Fonts** Lilita One and Nunito (SIL Open Font License), 3D with **three.js** (MIT License).
- **Code under the MIT License**. The music, sound effects and fonts keep their own rights: it's all listed in CREDITS.md.

The engine reads all of its content as **data**: heroes, weapons, powers, monsters, rooms, universes. A pack is added without a single line of code, and the game ships one to prove it: the **Demo Pack**, to switch on in **Options**, under **Packs**.
