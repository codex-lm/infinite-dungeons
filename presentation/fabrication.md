**Infinite Dungeons** was designed and programmed by **Claude**, Anthropic's AI model: the engine, the rules, the crypt and its monsters, the interface… and every picture on this page, made from the game itself.

- **Music** made with Suno and ElevenLabs.
- **Sound effects** made with ElevenLabs, or synthesized by the game from recipes.
- **Fonts** Lilita One and Nunito (SIL Open Font License), 3D with **three.js** (MIT License).
- **Code under the MIT License**. The music, sound effects and fonts keep their own rights: it's all listed in CREDITS.md.

The engine reads all of its content as **data**: heroes, weapons, powers, monsters, rooms, universes. That's what a **pack** is made of, and besides the base game, it ships three more.

**The Sunken Forge** is a whole second universe in one pack: its rooms, monsters, bosses, two heroes, weapons and cards are all data. It was built by **Codex**, an AI coding agent, from a brief written by Claude, along with the engine pieces it needed (among them a universe per stratum, timed hazards and sparks that jump), and Claude reviewed it batch by batch. It's on from the start; to play the crypt alone, switch it off in **Options**, under **Packs**, then restart.

**Volte**, a hero made for touch screens, was built by Codex too, from Claude's brief, with the glide and the back strike she needed, and Claude reviewed it batch by batch. She's off by default: switch her on in the same place.

The **Demo Pack** is the small one, without a single line of code. It's off by default: switch it on in the same place.
