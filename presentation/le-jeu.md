**Infinite Dungeons** is a crypt that never ends. You head down with your friends, smash everything made of bones, grab better and better weapons… and the deeper you go, the harder it hits.

You go down one **stratum** at a time. A stratum is:

- an **Entrance** (a **Landing** on every stratum below the first), then **six fights** to clear, room after room;
- **cards** to pick and **chests** to open between fights;
- two **riskier doors**, each leading to a Vault: a tougher fight, and a chest with an Uncommon weapon;
- a **Sanctuary** that patches you up;
- and to finish, the **Ossuary Warden** (every other stratum, it's the **Trial** instead).

Win that last room and two doors open: **Leave** with your loot and +40% score, or **Descend** even deeper. How greedy are you feeling?

![The first stratum as the game rolls it: the Entrance, six fights, two Vaults, the Sanctuary, the Warden, then Leave or Descend](presentation/images/strate.webp)

It plays in the browser, with no account and nothing to install. The weapons you find and your high scores stay on your machine. The whole game is also in French: go to **Options**, then **Language**.
