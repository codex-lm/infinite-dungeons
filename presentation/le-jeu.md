**Infinite Dungeons** is a dungeon that never ends. You head down with your friends through the **Smoldering Crypt**, full of bones that won't stay put, **The Sunken Forge**, all lava and oversized tools, and, from the third stratum, **Toadstool Hollow**, a damp cave of giant mushrooms, frogs and a king covered in warts. Smash everything, grab better and better weapons… and the deeper you go, the harder it hits.

You go down one **stratum** at a time, from one world to the next. A stratum is:

- an **Entrance** on the first stratum (on the deeper ones, a **Landing** in the crypt, **The Hoist** in the forge or **The Root Well** in the Hollow), then **six fights** to clear, room after room;
- **cards** to pick and **chests** to open between fights;
- two **riskier doors**, each leading to a **Vault** (in the forge, **The Ingot Hoard** or **The Slag Pit**; in the Hollow, **The Jar Stash** or **The Nest**): a tougher fight, then a chest (an Uncommon weapon, a bonus and some gold) and a card to pick;
- a **Sanctuary** that patches you up (**The Hearth** in the forge, **The Spring** in the Hollow);
- and to finish, a final fight. In the crypt, it's the **Ossuary Warden**. In the forge, it's the **Trial** on the second stratum, then **The Forgemaster** and the **Trial by Fire**. In the Hollow, it's **The Toad King**, in his own court.

Win that last room and two doors open: **Leave** with your loot and +40% score, or **Descend** even deeper. How greedy are you feeling?

It plays in the browser, with no account and nothing to install, on a computer or by finger on a phone or a tablet. The weapons you find and your high scores stay on your machine. The whole game is also in French: go to **Options**, then **Language**.

**Want to keep it?** The same game also installs from [codex-lm.github.io/infinite-dungeons](https://codex-lm.github.io/infinite-dungeons/), on a phone, a tablet or a computer: in your browser's menu, choose the option to install it (**Install app** in Chrome on Android; on an iPhone or an iPad, **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**). It gets its own icon, opens without the browser's bars (full screen on Android) and plays even offline (on an iPhone or an iPad, open it once from its icon with internet first). Nothing else is downloaded: it's the same web page, kept by your browser, with no more access to your device than here. The installed game keeps its own saves.
