Six weapons, two ways to hit. From afar with the **Repeater Bow** and the **Scatter Crossbow**, or up close with the **Sword**, the **Axe**, the **Spear** and the **Mace**. Each has its own rhythm: the sword chains three blows, the axe sweeps wide, the spear reaches far, and the mace is slow but stuns whatever it hits. The spear and the mace only turn up from the third floor down.

Weapons drop from crates, chests, elites and the big monsters, in three rarities:

- **Common**, in gray: the basics.
- **Uncommon**, in green: a little more damage.
- **Rare**, in blue: more still, plus an effect, **of Embers** (sets what it hits ablaze) or **of Frost** (slows it down). It lands under a column of light you can see from across the room.

![The six weapons, then the same axe as Common, Uncommon and Rare, each under its column of light](presentation/images/armes.webp)

Walk up to a weapon: a label compares its damage, speed and reach with what you're holding. If it's better, take it. Your old weapon stays on the floor, for a friend.
