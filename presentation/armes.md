Fifteen weapons, two ways to hit. From afar with the **Repeater Bow**, the **Scatter Crossbow**, the **Rivet Crossbow**, the **Spark Caster** and the **Blowpipe**. Up close with the **Sword**, the **Axe**, the **Spear**, the **Mace**, the **Forge Gauntlets**, the **Glaive**, the **Forge Hammer**, the **Twin Daggers**, the **Scythe** and the **Flail**.

Each has its own rhythm. The sword chains three blows, the axe sweeps wide, the spear reaches far, and the mace is slow but stuns what it hits and smashes right through a guard. The gauntlets jab fast and finish with a big one, the glaive slashes wide from far off, and the hammer is heavy: every blow that lands sends out a shockwave. The daggers cut four times in a breath, the last one twice as hard; the scythe reaps everything in front and to the sides; the flail swings a full circle around you. At range, a rivet goes through two more enemies, a spark jumps to two more nearby, and the blowpipe spits darts that bounce off once.

The spear, the mace and the glaive only turn up from the third fight on, the Rivet Crossbow from the second and the Forge Hammer from the fifth. The four weapons of **Toadstool Hollow** only turn up from the third stratum. The forge's and the Hollow's weapons drop in every world.

Weapons drop from crates, chests, elites and the big monsters, in five rarities:

- **Common**, in gray: the basics.
- **Uncommon**, in green: a little more damage.
- **Rare**, in blue: more still, plus an effect: **of Embers** (sets what it hits ablaze), **of Frost** (slows it down), **of the Anvil** (direct hits set off a small blast), **of Lightning** (a bolt jumps to two more enemies) or **of the Leech** (kills heal you). It lands under a column of light you can see from across the room.
- **Mythic**, in purple, from the third stratum: more damage again, and an effect too.
- **Legendary**, in gold, from the fourth stratum: the strongest of all, with an effect, and rare even down there.

The **Toad King** gives a Mythic weapon to every hero, plus a Legendary for the team; the first **Big Bolete** of a stratum, a Mythic each; and the **Golden Chest** of the Hollow, one more.

Walk up to a weapon: a label compares its damage, speed and reach with what you're holding. If it's better, take it. Your old weapon stays on the floor, for a friend.
