Eleven weapons, two ways to hit. From afar with the **Repeater Bow**, the **Scatter Crossbow**, the **Rivet Crossbow** and the **Spark Caster**. Up close with the **Sword**, the **Axe**, the **Spear**, the **Mace**, the **Forge Gauntlets**, the **Glaive** and the **Forge Hammer**.

Each has its own rhythm. The sword chains three blows, the axe sweeps wide, the spear reaches far, and the mace is slow but stuns what it hits and smashes right through a guard. The gauntlets jab fast and finish with a big one, the glaive slashes wide from far off, and the hammer is heavy: every blow that lands sends out a shockwave. At range, a rivet goes through two more enemies, and a spark jumps to two more nearby.

The spear, the mace and the glaive only turn up from the third fight on, the Rivet Crossbow from the second and the Forge Hammer from the fifth. The forge's weapons drop in the crypt too.

Weapons drop from crates, chests, elites and the big monsters, in three rarities:

- **Common**, in gray: the basics.
- **Uncommon**, in green: a little more damage.
- **Rare**, in blue: more still, plus an effect: **of Embers** (sets what it hits ablaze), **of Frost** (slows it down) or **of the Anvil** (direct hits set off a small blast). It lands under a column of light you can see from across the room.

![The eleven weapons, from the crypt and the forge, then the same axe as Common, Uncommon and Rare, the Uncommon and the Rare each under its column of light](presentation/images/armes.webp)

Walk up to a weapon: a label compares its damage, speed and reach with what you're holding. If it's better, take it. Your old weapon stays on the floor, for a friend.
