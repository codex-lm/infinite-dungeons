Choose your hero. There are five for up to four players, so everyone gets their own (two of the same still works, if you insist):

- **Ranger**: quick and precise, he peppers everything with the **Repeater Bow**. His power, **Volley**, fires three rapid bursts of five bolts.
- **Bastion**: solid as a wall, **Scatter Crossbow** in hand. His **Shockwave** knocks back everything that gets too close.
- **Duelist**: sword in hand and a grin on his face. His **Whirlwind** spins three times, blade out, and every melee kill brings his dodge back sooner.
- **Bruiser**: fists first, questions later. She jabs fast with the **Forge Gauntlets**, her **Fist Flurry** throws six quick punches in front of her, and every melee kill gives her a short shield.
- **Sparkler**: her **Spark Caster** shoots sparks that jump from one enemy to the next, and her **Discharge** sets off a close burst that leaps through the crowd.

The last two come from **The Sunken Forge**. Everyone has a **dodge** to slip through blows, and a **power** that recharges on its own.

One more, made for fingers on a tablet: **Volte**, the gliding duelist. Hold the dodge and she glides for up to a second, steered by your thumb around a foe; let go when the gold lights up behind its back, and she strikes it hard and gets her glide straight back. She comes as a pack: switch her on in **Options**, then **Packs**.
